Meet Dr. Nikhil Shah

Dr. Nikhil Shah is a distinguished consultant homeopath and wellness coach with over 40 years of clinical experience. A graduate in Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS), Dr. Shah has dedicated his career to helping patients achieve optimal health through the science of homeopathy.

His approach combines classical homeopathic principles with a deep understanding of modern lifestyle challenges. Every patient who walks through the doors of Alka Clinic receives a thorough evaluation — not just of their symptoms, but of their entire physical, mental, and emotional constitution.

Our Philosophy

At Alka Clinic, we believe that true healing comes from within. Homeopathy works by stimulating the body's natural defense mechanisms, encouraging the system to heal itself from the root cause. This results in lasting health improvements without the risk of side effects.

  • Treat the Patient, Not Just the Disease — Every individual is unique, and their treatment should reflect their specific constitution and needs.
  • Root Cause Resolution — We focus on identifying and addressing the underlying cause of illness rather than suppressing symptoms.
  • Safe & Natural Healing — Our medicines are derived from natural sources and are safe for patients of all ages, from infants to the elderly.
  • Holistic Wellness — Beyond prescriptions, we offer comprehensive wellness coaching including dietary guidance, lifestyle modifications, and stress management techniques.
  • Accessible Healthcare — With both in-person and telephonic consultation options, quality homeopathic care is available to patients regardless of their location.

Areas of Clinical Excellence

Over the decades, Dr. Shah has developed particular expertise in treating chronic lifestyle diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, and thyroid disorders. He is also widely recognized for his successful treatment of stubborn skin conditions like psoriasis, eczema, and chronic allergies — conditions where conventional medicine often falls short.

His weight management programs combine homeopathic remedies with personalized diet plans and body composition analysis, helping patients achieve sustainable, healthy weight goals without crash diets or harmful supplements.

Our Journey
