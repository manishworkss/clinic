The Complete Solution for Your Health

Founded in 1985, Alka Clinic gives you a one-stop solution for all your healthcare and vitality needs. Under the visionary medical guidance of Dr. Nikhil Shah, the clinic has treated over 50,000 patients across Gujarat, India, and internationally.

Ask for With / Without Medication

We respect your preferences and individual constitution: you can opt for classical homeopathic therapeutic medication, or choose dedicated nutritional therapy, diet counseling, and lifestyle wellness coaching without pills.

Our Clinical Focus Areas

We provide specialized treatment and root-cause solutions for the following problems:

  • Obesity & Weight Management: Weight loss, weight gain, body fat analysis, and maintenance diets.
  • Chronic Dermatological Diseases: Psoriasis, stubborn eczema, allergies, lichen planus, vitiligo, and cystic acne.
  • Lifestyle Disorders: Diabetes mellitus, hypertension (BP), thyroid imbalance, and bronchial asthma.
  • Children's Health: Pediatric immunity, growth, recurrent coughs, colds, and skin rashes.
  • Hair & Scalp Care: Severe hairfall, alopecia areata, chronic dandruff, and premature thinning.
  • Digestive & Gut Health: Chronic acidity, GERD, gas, constipation, IBS, and healthy breakfast solutions.
  • Joint & Bone Health: Arthritis, spondylitis, sciatica, and joint stiffness.
  • Complete Wellness & Immunity: Total vitality, sports nutrition, energy, fitness, and looking good & feeling good.
